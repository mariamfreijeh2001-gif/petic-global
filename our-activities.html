Roads Construction

East Trading & Manufacturing Company has worked hard to maintain its reputation in the construction quality of asphalt roads and highways. It has successfully completed a range of large-scale road projects, from large highways to small commercial site developments, approaching every project with dedicated efficiency, technology and a special focus on safety.

Civil Works & Construction

East Trading & Manufacturing Company holds a portfolio of strategic investment in construction equipment, property and a substantial fleet of mobile construction equipment in Tanzania. This complements the company’s multi-domestic growth strategy, which focuses on its operating hubs in East African countries.

Agriculture

AgriPro is a leading agriculture company dedicated to transforming arable lands into highly productive and sustainable agricultural ventures. With a strong commitment to innovation, technology and environmental responsibility, we provide comprehensive solutions for land development and irrigation systems.

Oil & Gas

A well-resourced, full-service oilfield supply company serving the oil & gas industry, with asset integrity, material supply and static equipment rehabilitation services. Our clients include major oil and gas companies, projects and contractors in Yemen.
