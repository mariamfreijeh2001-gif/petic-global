Overview

All phases of road construction.

East Trading & Manufacturing Company has worked hard to maintain its reputation in the construction quality of asphalt roads and highways. It has successfully completed a range of large-scale road projects, from large highways to small commercial site developments, approaching every project with dedicated efficiency, technology and a special focus on safety.

The company performs all phases of road construction, including earthwork, utilities, drainage, concrete and asphalt paving, bridge construction, as well as aggregate production.

What makes the company special is not just its versatility: the company has undertaken many public and private sector projects worth multi-million dollars, enabling the group to operate with honesty, integrity and higher standards.
