Oil & gas.

Asset integrity, material supply and static equipment rehabilitation for the oil & gas industry, serving major companies, projects and contractors.

“Chase the vision, not the money. The money will end up following you.”

Asset integrity

Safe operation of ageing assets.

One of the major challenges facing the oil and gas industry today is to ensure the safe operation of their ageing assets. Asset integrity entails making sure an asset can perform its required function effectively and safely.

Our asset integrity partner, Mega, has a well trained and experienced team certified to API (510, 570, 580, 653), CSWIP welding & painting, NACE and ASNT (UT, RT, MT, PT, VT), supported by specialized tools and equipment. Mega is accredited by the Egyptian Accreditation Council (EGAC) under ISO/IEC 17020 as a Type C inspection body in the field of in-service inspection.

Material supply services

PETIC GLOBAL is a well-resourced and full-service oilfield supply company serving the oil & gas industry in the country. Our clients include major oil and gas companies, projects and contractors in Yemen. Our company has associations with renowned manufacturers, major mills and suppliers across the world.

PETIC GLOBAL is an oilfield supplier you can trust for any urgent and important oil & gas project supply. Operations are conducted from purpose-built facilities in Yemen, comprising offices, workshops, warehousing and covered storage areas, from where a wide range of products and services covering a large spectrum of the energy industry are supplied. Products range from materials required for the drilling of wells to equipment required for large downstream projects.

PETIC GLOBAL Stocks and Supplies are committed to client satisfaction in every aspect of our business, guided by our core values of integrity and teamwork.

Team capabilities & qualification

Well trained and certified maintenance teams who have gained various maintenance technologies by handling a diversity of process types (oil, gas, LNG and more) with their related operation techniques and safety hazards, inside and outside the oil & gas sector, for railways, the industrial sector and more.

Static equipment rehabilitation

A significant market share as a unique service provider for boiler and heater revamping, separator fabrication and heat exchangers in oil & gas fields, including water and fire-tube boiler revamping, supported by a specialized workshop complying with the ASME code.
