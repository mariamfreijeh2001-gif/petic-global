Who we are

Established worldwide, rooted in the Gulf and Africa.

Sheik Salah has been in worldwide business, establishing various companies in the Middle East and Africa, providing quality construction projects and engineering services in the sector of oil & gas.

At the beginning, the Petic group invested heavily in the Gulf States and Tanzania by building infrastructure and road projects. In its next phase the group moved into manufacturing household and pharmaceutical products in Rwanda, and gas cylinders in Uganda.

The group is now planning to represent Chinese car manufacturers by creating an assembling workshop in Africa.

East Africa

East Trading & Manufacturing Company LTD

Kisutu Street 2423, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania

East Trading & Manufacturing Company has been set up in Tanzania to provide quality construction services to real estate contractors, construction companies, and the civil engineering and infrastructure industries. The company established a successful track record through commitments to its customers within the operations and construction domain.

After gaining extensive experience in building services, the company is moving forward to undertake complex and challenging civil engineering and building projects, extending its services from innovative technical concepts and front-end engineering designs to construction management, installation, commissioning and long-term operation and maintenance.

The company holds a portfolio of strategic investments in the construction sector, owning a substantial fleet of mobile plant in the Gulf States and East Africa. This complements the company’s multi-domestic growth strategy, which focuses its operating hubs in both regions.

Our mission

Contributing to the development of the Gulf States and East Africa.

Our mission is to participate in and contribute to the development and enrichment of services in the Gulf States and East Africa region, offering communities solutions in the construction sector and providing high quality engineering services.

The company is fully committed to looking after the interests of its customers, employees, shareholders and communities. The group conducts its business ethically and profitably as a responsible corporate citizen. To accomplish our mission, we continually:

  • Enhance our competence with powerful, qualified engineering minds, enabling good communication at all levels to improve the operating environment.
  • Expand our presence in existing markets through fair pricing, respecting deadlines, quality products and good-value solutions, using advanced and creative technological approaches with continuous improvement of our operating systems.
  • Energize our employees by continuously promoting a culture that recognizes performance and valuable contributions, and celebrates successes.
